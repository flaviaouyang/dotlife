# Sample data

Half a year in the life of Alice, yes, the Alice of Alice and Bob, finally with a life outside cryptography papers. In 2026 she is a cryptographer in London: she climbs, cycles, learns Rust, shares a flat (and a key) with Bob, and is fairly sure Eve has been reading her messages.

Every value is made up. The files are generated by a script, so please don't edit them by hand.
